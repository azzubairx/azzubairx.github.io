Browsers

Your browser sees everything you do online, which makes it one of the most consequential choices you can make.

7 tools

Brave

BeginnerFreeOpen sourceWindows · macOS · Linux · AndroidChromium

A familiar Chromium experience with privacy and security on by default. The built-in extras are easy to turn off and don’t weaken what makes it strong.

Firefox

BeginnerFreeOpen sourceWindows · macOS · Linux · AndroidGecko

The strongest non-Chromium option on desktop. Solid out of the box, and one of the best browsers anywhere once hardened.

Helium

IntermediateFreeOpen sourceWindows · macOS · LinuxChromium

A lightweight, open-source Chromium browser built for speed and a distraction-free interface, with user privacy as a design goal.

LibreWolf

IntermediateFreeOpen sourceWindows · macOS · LinuxGecko

A community Firefox build that ships most recommended hardening settings by default, so you skip the manual configuration.

Tor Browser

AdvancedFreeOpen sourceWindows · macOS · Linux · AndroidGecko

Routes your traffic through the Tor network — strong for full anonymity and censorship circumvention, at the cost of speed.

Mullvad Browser

IntermediateFreeOpen sourceWindows · macOS · LinuxGecko

Built with the Tor Project to apply fingerprinting protections to ordinary browsing, without routing through Tor. A strong desktop pick for serious privacy, though it may break some sites.

Safari

BeginnerFreeProprietarymacOS · iOSWebKit

A reasonable default for Apple users, with privacy basics built in and Lockdown Mode on iOS. Pair it with a strong blocker for better coverage.

Browser extensions

Small add-ons that cut tracking noticeably and improve your overall security posture.

5 tools

AdGuard

BeginnerFree/paidOpen sourceChrome · Firefox · Edge · Safari

A reliable, low-friction way to block ads and trackers — a good fit if you want effective blocking that rarely breaks sites.

Ghostery

BeginnerFree/paidOpen sourceFirefox · Chrome · Edge · Safari

An open-source tracker and ad blocker with a beginner-friendly interface and simple visual cues for what it blocks.

DuckDuckGo Privacy

BeginnerFreeOpen sourceChrome · Firefox · Edge · Safari

A stripped-down extension that blocks trackers and shows a privacy grade for the site you’re on.

uBlock Origin

IntermediateFreeOpen sourceFirefox · Chrome · Edge · Opera

The strongest ad and malware blocker available. Very light on resources and highly customisable.

uBlock Origin Lite

IntermediateFreeOpen sourceChrome · Edge · Safari (MV3)

The Manifest V3-compatible build. It blocks less than the full version but is still a meaningful improvement over nothing.

Search engines

A search engine that respects privacy answers your questions without keeping a history of them.

4 tools

Brave Search

BeginnerFree/paidProprietaryWeb · independent index

Runs its own independent index instead of leaning on Google — a strong option for a familiar search experience without tracking.

DuckDuckGo

BeginnerFreeProprietaryWeb · aggregated results

Pulls results mainly from Bing without passing your identity along — an easy entry point for people leaving Google.

Kagi

BeginnerPaidProprietaryWeb · independent index

A paid search engine with its own index, no ads and no tracking. Subscriptions remove the conflict of interest.

SearXNG

IntermediateFreeOpen sourceWeb · self-hosted

An open-source metasearch engine that queries dozens of sources without logging your searches. Self-hostable.

DNS providers

DNS turns every domain you visit into an address. Your default provider logs all of it, and switching is the easiest win available.

3 tools

NextDNS

IntermediateFree/paidProprietaryAll devicesAffiliate

Encrypted, fully configurable DNS. Build your own blocklists and filtering across all your devices.

AdGuard DNS

BeginnerFree/paidOpen sourceAll devices

An encrypted resolver with ad and tracker blocking built in, no software to install — low-effort network-level protection.

Quad9

BeginnerFreeOpen sourceAll devices

A free, privacy-respecting encrypted resolver with malware blocking, run by a Swiss non-profit.

VPN providers

Useful for hiding your traffic from your ISP and changing your IP address.

3 tools

ProtonVPN

BeginnerFree/paidOpen sourceWindows · macOS · Linux · Android

A well-reviewed, open-source VPN from Proton with a generous free tier: no data cap, no ads.

Mullvad VPN

BeginnerPaidOpen sourceWindows · macOS · Linux · Android · iOS

A VPN built around anonymous account numbers and cash payments, with regular audits and a clear no-logs policy. Not free, but one of the clearest options in terms of transparency.

Windscribe

BeginnerFree/paidOpen sourceWindows · macOS · Linux · Android

A generous free tier with a configurable firewall — a strong pick if you want flexibility without a high price.

Messaging apps

A conversation is only as private as the app carrying it. These are the ones worth trusting.

4 tools

Signal

BeginnerFreeOpen sourceWindows · macOS · Linux · Android

The most trusted messaging app around: strong encryption, a simple interface, and usernames instead of phone numbers.

SimpleX

AdvancedFreeOpen sourceWindows · macOS · Linux · Android

Decentralised messaging that needs no phone number, email or permanent username. Best for sensitive conversations.

Element

IntermediateFreeOpen sourceWindows · macOS · Linux · Android · iOS

A client for Matrix, an open, decentralised messaging protocol. It gives you end-to-end encrypted rooms and more control over your data, at the cost of some setup.

Telegram

BeginnerFree/paidOpen sourceWindows · macOS · Linux · Android

Flexible and feature-rich. Not end-to-end encrypted by default, but the best option for large communities and big file transfers.

Email & aliasing

Use services without handing over your real address, which limits the damage when a site leaks data.

4 tools

Proton Mail

BeginnerFree/paidOpen sourceWindows · macOS · Linux · AndroidWebAffiliate

Zero-access encryption plus end-to-end encryption between Proton users, with servers in Switzerland.

DuckDuckGo Mail

BeginnerFreeProprietaryWeb

Creates a separate address for every site, keeping your real inbox private and cutting hidden email tracking.

Proton Pass (Aliasing)

BeginnerFree/paidOpen sourceAll systemsAffiliate

A password manager with aliasing built in, powered by SimpleLogin — the most integrated way to create throwaway addresses.

addy.io

BeginnerFree/paidOpen sourceWeb · browser extension

An open-source aliasing service with one of the most generous free tiers (unlimited aliases) — a strong SimpleLogin alternative.

Passwords & 2FA

Reusing passwords is the biggest security risk most people carry. Use a strong, trusted manager.

4 tools

Bitwarden

BeginnerFree/paidOpen sourceAll systems

A trusted cloud password manager with zero-knowledge encryption: only you can open the vault. Self-hosting is supported.

KeePassXC

AdvancedFreeOpen sourceWindows · macOS · LinuxLocal

A fully local password manager: your data lives in an encrypted file on your own device, with no forced cloud sync.

Proton Authenticator

BeginnerFreeOpen sourceWindows · macOS · Linux · Android

Free, open-source two-factor authentication from Proton, with mobile and desktop apps and encrypted backup of your codes.

2FAS

BeginnerFreeOpen sourcemacOS · Android

An excellent authenticator: no account required, works offline, and supports encrypted cloud backups.
